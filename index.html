📖 About This Book

A comprehensive companion to Studio Bones' Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood — covering all sixty-four episodes, the characters, themes, and artistry of what many consider the greatest anime series ever made.

From the Elric Brothers' desperate quest for the Philosopher's Stone to the secrets of the Homunculi, from the scars of Ishval to the truth behind the Gate — every chapter of this masterpiece is explored in depth. Includes character studies, philosophical analysis, comparison with the 2003 series, and the legacy of Hiromu Arakawa's original manga.
